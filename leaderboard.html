Retrieval: MediaFind's on-device memory search (bge-m3 + BM25 lexical fusion) ranks the benchmark's captions and emails, bge-reranker-v2-m3 reranks the whole text of the top-50 items, and that ranking is reciprocal-rank fused with the lexical ranking of a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rewrite of the question. Memory: each photo/video carries a memory card written by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash from its caption, the person's emails (a trip/event timeline extracted from all emails) and same-day items, plus a dense visual description of the raw photo or video frames by DeepSeek V4 Flash Vision. Answering: a map-reduce over the top-25 items, in which DeepSeek V4.1 Flash labels the relevant items per batch of ten and GPT-5.4 mini (reasoning effort minimal) answers from the kept items' full text (for list questions the reduce step also sees the ten top-ranked items); QS uses gpt-5-mini. Oracle = gold evidence only, no haystack. Approximate context depth is shown in each column header.

SGM

Raw (real images/video)

Why SGM, not raw? Raw (real images/video) edges out SGM at the Oracle ceiling. But that advantage collapses under realistic conditions: as the haystack fills with distractors, raw degrades and even fails (payload/context limits), and under agentic retrieval the gap is stark — every "w/o SGM" (raw) agent lands far below its SGM run. SGM is the representation that holds up once there is noise under realistic conditions.
